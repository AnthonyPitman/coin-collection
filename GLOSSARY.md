# Coin Collection

A collection records quantities of coins owned and supports counts, face value totals, and breakdowns by coin characteristics.

## Language

**Coin type**:
A category of coin in the collection, initially penny, nickel, dime, quarter, half dollar, Eisenhower dollar, Susan B. Anthony dollar, Sacagawea dollar, presidential dollar, or American Innovation dollar. Different coin types can share a denomination.

**Denomination**:
The monetary unit of a coin, such as a US cent, dime, or quarter.

**Year**:
The coin's issue year, or unknown when it cannot be established. For a dual-date commemorative inscription such as 1776–1976, the year is 1976; it does not assert the actual production year.

**Date inscription**:
The date text displayed on a coin, including commemorative dual dates such as 1776–1976. A dual date describes one coin issue, rather than a range of collection years.

**Mint**:
The facility where a coin was produced, such as San Francisco.

**Mint mark**:
The marking observed on a coin that identifies its mint, such as P, D, or S. No mark means the coin has no mint mark; unknown means the mark has not been established.

**Design**:
The distinguishing artwork or subject of a coin, such as the wheat reverse on a US cent or North Carolina on a state quarter.

**Design family**:
A grouping of related coin designs, such as State Quarters, wheat reverse cents, or Native American dollar designs within the Sacagawea coin type.

**Design subject**:
The specific subject within a design family, where applicable, such as North Carolina within State Quarters.

**Coin catalog**:
Reference information about coin types and their known years, mint marks, and designs. A coin can be recorded even when its characteristics are not recognized by the catalog.

**Quantity**:
The number of coins owned with the same recorded characteristics.

**Coin count**:
The sum of quantities across a collection or a selected set of entries, rather than the number of entries.

**Collection entry**:
A quantity of coins sharing a coin type, year, observed mint mark, and design family and subject where applicable. An explicit mint mark and no mint mark identify separate entries, even when their mint is the same.

**Face value**:
The monetary value assigned to a coin by its issuing authority, independent of its collectible value.

**Total face value**:
The sum of each entry's quantity multiplied by its denomination's face value, for a collection or a selected set of entries.
